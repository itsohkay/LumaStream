BEHIND THE SCREEN

Stories bring
us together.

LumaStream is a student-created entertainment discovery concept designed to bring different kinds of screen stories into one easy-to-explore space. It celebrates the variety of films, series, animation and documentaries that audiences enjoy.

The project demonstrates semantic HTML5, responsive CSS, Bootstrap components, JavaScript, arrays of objects, DOM interaction, form validation, Fetch API and browser localStorage.
